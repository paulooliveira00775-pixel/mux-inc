01 / THE STORY

A brand for the
things we imagine.

Mux is a fictional technology brand created to explore what personal technology could look and feel like. It’s a place for concept phones, tablets, software ideas, and the little design decisions that make a product feel special.

Nothing here needs a factory to begin. It starts with curiosity, a sketch, and the question: what if?
